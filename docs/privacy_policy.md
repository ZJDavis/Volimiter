# Volimiter Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Volimiter is an Android app that helps limit media volume when audio is playing through the device speaker.

## Information Volimiter Stores

Volimiter stores the following information locally on your device:

- Your selected maximum speaker volume level
- Whether Volimiter was active before the device restarted
- Your Volimiter PIN, protected using Android security features
- App state needed to support PIN-protected stopping and restart-after-boot behavior

This information is stored on your device and is used only to provide Volimiter’s core functionality.

## Information Volimiter Does Not Collect

Volimiter does not:

- Collect your name, email address, phone number, or account information
- Record audio
- Use the microphone
- Track your location
- Access your contacts, photos, messages, or files
- Send your PIN or volume settings to a server
- Use advertising identifiers
- Include advertising or analytics SDKs

## Device Permissions

Volimiter may request the following Android permissions or access:

- Foreground service access, so Volimiter can continue limiting volume while active
- Boot completed access, so Volimiter can restart after device reboot if it was previously active
- Device Admin access, if enabled by the user, to make Volimiter harder to uninstall while active

Device Admin access is optional, but uninstall-deterrence behavior requires it.

## Data Sharing

Volimiter does not sell, share, or transmit user data to third parties.

## Data Security

Volimiter stores app settings locally on the device. The PIN is protected using Android security features. No method of local storage is perfect, but Volimiter is designed not to transmit this information off the device.

## Parent-Managed or Self-Control Use

Volimiter may be used by a parent, guardian, or device owner to help manage speaker volume on a device. The person enabling Volimiter is responsible for ensuring that they have the authority to manage the device.

## Data Deletion

Because Volimiter stores data locally, users can delete Volimiter data by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app after Device Admin access has been disabled.
